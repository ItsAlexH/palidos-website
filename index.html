Why Now

The structural barriers that made qualitative analysis inaccessible are dissolving. LLMs change the economics of text analysis.

The Bandwidth Problem

According to the Association for Institutional Research, 82% of IR offices operate with fewer than five full-time staff, with the average office sitting at just 3.5 FTEs. Core data demands — compliance reporting, non-compliance reporting, and routine decision support — consume 61% of total work output, leaving minimal bandwidth for deeper analytical work.

AIR National Survey of IR Offices, 2016 & 2024

The Technology Shift

As Carol Trosset observed in the AIR volume Using Mixed Methods in Institutional Research, "Much qualitative data goes unanalyzed because people are unfamiliar with appropriate analytical techniques. They type up lists of survey comments and stop."

Modern data analysis tools have now matured to the point where systematic qualitative analysis is viable for the first time at institutional scale, without requiring specialized methodological training or weeks of manual effort per report cycle.
